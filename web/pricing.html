Every plan generates every drawing — context, site, yield and site report, at any scale or style — from the same data, drawn the same way, and shows it on the page. A free account downloads its PDF with a watermark; without an account there is no PDF. CAD is the drawing's geometry as DXF on the MGA2020 grid and as DWG. Data is the yield's CSV and JSON, and the site report's JSON.

One address is all of that for the one address, for 30 days. Generate any drawing of it, then press UNLOCK THIS ADDRESS under the result. The subscription is every address, while it runs.

Every drawing is made from open public data as it is available when the drawing is made. Site Analyst does not guarantee that data is complete, current or correct, and a gap in it is not a fault in the drawing — read a drawing in full before paying for it. See the terms of use.
