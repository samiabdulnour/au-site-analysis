HOW IT WORKS PLANS

A free account downloads the PDF, and buying an address or the subscription starts from it.

Every drawing is free to generate and to see. Pay to use them: the clean PDF, and the CAD and data behind it.

Every plan draws every sheet, at any scale or style. CAD is the geometry as DXF on the MGA2020 grid and as DWG; data is the yield's CSV and JSON and the site report's JSON.

One address unlocks everything for that address for 30 days: press UNLOCK THIS ADDRESS under any drawing of it. The subscription covers every address while it runs.

The one-address price is a launch price while Site Analyst is new; it will rise. An address already unlocked stays unlocked for its 30 days.

Drawings come from open public data, with no guarantee it is complete, current or correct, so read one in full before paying. See the terms of use.
