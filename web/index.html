HOW IT WORKS PLANS

Give it a NSW street address and it draws the site-analysis sheets an architect prepares for a development application — the lot on its cadastre and aerial, zoning and height controls, heritage, transport, walking catchments — entirely from government open data. Out come a print-ready A1 PDF and the same geometry as CAD. How it works.

Sign in, or make a free account: free PDFs carry a watermark. Unlocking a site, or the subscription, takes it off and adds the CAD and data files.
