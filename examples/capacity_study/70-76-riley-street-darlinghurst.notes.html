THE SITE AND ITS NEIGHBOURS

70-76 Riley Street is the subject of SSD-125616738, a State significant development application (HDA Housing) at the Prepare EIS stage: demolition of all structures and a 12-storey residential tower of 100 units, 15% of them affordable, over ground-floor commercial space and a basement car park.

Likelihood of redevelopment around the site in the short to medium term:

High. 93-95 Crown Street: a boarding house of 16 rooms and 210 m² of commercial space approved behind the retained terrace facades, marketed with its consent in 2021. 54-56 Riley Street with 1 Crown Lane: a 6-storey building of 16 apartments (D/2018/355) was lodged and the land marketed as a development site; the currency of that consent is to be confirmed.

Medium. 91-107 Riley Street opposite: three single titles of 1,625 m² in all, one of them a car park, for which a 13-storey and a 5-storey block of 67 apartments over a supermarket was lodged in 2012 and never built. 62-64 Riley Street, a commercial strata of five lots from 1999; 58 and 60 Riley Street, two small single titles; 4-16 Yurong Street, a 1,320 m² single title. Each needs an amalgamation or a collective sale to make a tower site.

Low. 66-68 Riley Street, a 21-apartment building of 2011; the three new buildings east of Crown Lane; the heritage terraces of Stanley, Crown and Riley Streets and the Lord Roberts Hotel; the large schemes at 77 Riley Street (112 lots), 18-20 Yurong Street (102) and 60-70 William Street (92); the William Street frontage, whose lots sit over the stratum excised for the Cross City Tunnel in 2003; 50 Riley Street, refurbished under a $3.9 M consent in 2022; and every small site under 250 m², which has little ability to develop above its current status.

The block's capacity, then, is the site itself and the two small sites to its north. The rest of its frontage is settled for the medium term.
